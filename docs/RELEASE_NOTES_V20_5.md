# Ace's CFB Toolkit v20.5

## Release Notes

### Roster Generator — New Tool

- Create fresh fictional rosters for one or more teams during Preseason or Week 0, keeping each team's position counts intact.
- Choose from seven roster calibers, from From Nothing to All-Time Team. Separately choose roster age, development potential, offensive/defensive schemes and appearance preferences.
- Added Run-First Defense, favoring interior strength, run-stopping linebackers and run-support safeties without excluding other archetypes.
- Shift talent between position groups without raising the roster's average rating. Talent shares edit independently and must total 100% before saving.
- Generate new names, faces, builds, ratings, abilities, development traits, skill caps and playing styles, with rare suffixes and hyphenated surnames.
- Expanded variety using another 11,730 game-generated players: more names, faces, verified face/name pairings and observed ability/potential combinations.
- Most players use a Balanced athleticism profile, with smaller chances for specialized athletes and rare size Outliers. New Field Savants favor reading the game over execution; Drill Specialists favor practiced skills over awareness. From Nothing and FCS Call-Up favor smaller builds.
- Equipment uses the Equipment Randomizer's normal generation from the active save's top 20 eligible donors per position, without enabling optional mod additions.
- Long-snap and dealbreaker fixes run automatically; Standard jersey numbering runs last without team traditions or retired-number protection.
- New players receive fresh broadcast identities, healthy injury/wear-and-tear state, coherent hometown/pipeline and eligibility histories, and observed handedness with matching QB equipment orientation.
- Captains are reassigned automatically. Existing statistics, awards and depth charts stay untouched; reorder your depth chart in-game afterward.
- Added 565 fictional player backstories, including campus life, relationships, hobbies and 15 college-football easter eggs. Stories with specific details match the finished player’s class, ability, build, personality or position. Stories are retained in Toolkit reviews, History and CSV reports, not written into an in-game biography field.
- Review complete player cards or tables before applying. Apply creates a backup and verifies the exact preview before replacing the save.

### Commentary IDs

- Added 23 verified surname/commentary-ID pairs from the generated-player samples.

### Dealbreaker Fixer

- Ideal recruiting pitches now stay in sync when a player's dealbreaker changes, including the fixes run during roster generation.

### General

- Distinct icons for Roster Generator, NIL Toggle and Equipment Patcher.
- CSV report names identify Preview versus Apply runs, with Open File Location actions.
- Additional roster-generation diagnostics in reports, including donor sources, talent targets, athleticism profiles and backstory eligibility.

### Important Notes

- Roster Generator's targets refer to average player OVR, not the game's displayed team rating.
- Selected teams' NIL players are replaced too; the tool asks for confirmation. Existing statistics and roster links are retained.
- Test generated rosters in-game on a copied save before continuing a dynasty.
- Personal settings, custom artwork, saves and reports are not included in the installer. Nothing is published by the local packaging process.
