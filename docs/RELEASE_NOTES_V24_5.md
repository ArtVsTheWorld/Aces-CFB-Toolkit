### Release Notes

Equipment Tools

- Fixed equipment storage errors on heavily edited dynasty saves, including Quickstart runs with GameDay Gear enabled
- Reduced the save space used by equipment without dropping worn items
- Runs now skip players who cannot fit in the remaining equipment storage instead of failing the entire preview
- Simplified skipped-player messages to show a count
- Fixed invisible torsos caused by Relaxed Tees with incompatible rolled jerseys, preserving the jersey style and replacing the tee with a matching sleeveless compression undershirt

Equipment Randomizer

- Split linebacker donor settings into MIKE, SAM and WILL, and safety settings into FS and SS
- Added separate K and P donor source controls
- Choose exactly which positions can supply equipment to each receiving position, including its own position
- Selecting only WR as a donor source now lets CB, FS and SS pull exclusively from WRs
- Copy donor source settings to multiple positions from the same settings window
- Added Select Top # and Select All shortcuts for specific donors, while keeping selections from other positions
- Specific donors now default to Highest Overall, with Team as the second sort option
- Team sorting now orders players by team, highest overall, then alphabetical name
- Cleaned up donor settings alignment and made the current position clearer when copying sources

Equipment Randomizer and Equipment Quickstart

- Waist playcall bands and waist coaches are now restricted to QBs, including equipment copied from donors

Equipment Patcher

- Added Remove Specific Item(s), using the existing player filters
- Choose from 686 removable items with categories, search and alternate display names
- Includes turtlenecks, undershirts, leg sleeves, compression pants, facepaint, tattoos and supported modded accessories
- Helmets, facemasks and cleats remain protected, and removal is limited to categories that support an empty option
- Improved filter and button alignment in the removal settings

Equipment Mapper

- Empty equipment slots remain available for matching and replacement after unused empty entries have been cleaned up
