### Release Notes

General
- Mod requirements now show the mod name without a recommended version number
- Updated Jersey Renumber and Team Boost icons to match the other tools

Equipment Randomizer
- Added an optional ELJAX's Equipment Truck pool by ElJax YT with all three sleeve designs in four colors and both team-branded visors
- Choose allowed items and separate sleeve and visor chances in Configure Pool

Equipment Patcher
- Recolor Accessories now supports all four colors for Equipment Truck sleeves while preserving their design

Roster Generator
- Player cards now put key ratings, hometown, class, archetype, size, development, and captain status first. Technical details stay expandable
- Added a rare chance for two generated teammates to be brothers, sharing a last name, skin tone, and hometown
- Redistributes each regenerated team's existing NIL costs without increasing or reducing its total. Quarterbacks generally receive a larger share
- Added support for Play Now roster files. Only verified roster fields are changed; dynasty-only settings do not carry over, and roster depth charts are left intact
- Fixed generated Play Now rosters silently failing to load by updating the file's integrity information after generation

Equipment Mapper (NEW)
- Browse and search all saved gear, including uncataloged items and previously omitted facemasks, with brand, color, usage counts, and wearer lists
- Each item appears once, with compact side choices and saved configurations tucked away instead of repeated rows
- Queue precise replacements or copy individual items from a chosen player
- Copy or replace gloves, cleats, and arm sleeves as left/right pairs. Gloves start with paired copying enabled
- Set a replacement percentage per rule, applied separately to each team's matching players
- Saved configurations now explain their item settings instead of showing unexplained variants
- Coach wardrobe items are excluded, and category and brand filters use readable names
- Target teams, positions, selected players, or all matching players
- Replacements stay in the same exact slot, including left and right; unresolved items are visible but read-only
- Save mapping presets and review every change before Apply
- Uses the existing backup, write-verification, and rollback protections. Incompatible mappings must be resolved before Apply
