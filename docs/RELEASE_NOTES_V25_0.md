### Release Notes

General

- Improved tool loading times and reduced repeated work during Preview and Apply
- Faster repeat visits to equipment tools while the save is unchanged
- Simplified explanations and tooltips throughout the app, with clearer examples and searchable Help

Equipment Mapper

- Rebuilt the Mapper around Equipment, Players and Review pages, with cleaner spacing and alignment
- Choose existing and replacement items from the full equipment catalog instead of only items already in your save
- Select your installed mods first to show their supported equipment
- Added Assign and Remove actions, plus Frequency and Fill to Frequency controls
- Added an Only When Wearing filter for targeting equipment combinations, such as removing turtlenecks from players wearing Relaxed Tees
- Player lists and matching counts now follow your filters, with 100 players per page
- Added clearer rule summaries, duplication and links to conflicting rules
- Known equipment incompatibilities now show a red warning and ask for confirmation before applying
- Switching to Assign keeps the item you already selected

Equipment Randomizer and Equipment Quickstart

- Lowered the default Unlocked balaclava chance to 5%
- Added matching spats on both feet to the Unlocked pool, with a default 5% chance
- Elbow braces, elbow pads, String Towels and Wrist Brace Support are excluded from default pool selections
- OL, DT, K and P no longer receive pool gloves, balaclavas, turtlenecks or String Towels. Donor-copied gear is still allowed
- Taped hands remain available from pools for OL and DT, but not K or P
- No Drip chances are now individually configurable by position in Randomizer, with updated defaults that simplify linemen and specialists more often
- No Drip is now enabled by default in Randomizer; Quickstart uses the same updated defaults automatically
- Quickstart now preserves its selected donor players’ equipment

Equipment Patcher and Equipment Quickstart

- Fixed cutoff pants percentages rounding down too far on smaller position groups
- Updated default cutoff pants frequencies and enabled Fill to Frequency
- Added an optional setting to allow cutoff pants with leggings and leg sleeves, disabled by default with a red clipping warning
- Tattoo frequency now defaults to 30% with Fill to Frequency enabled

Equipment Compatibility

- Updated sleeve, undershirt and wrist/elbow compatibility across the equipment tools
- Black Short Arm Tape / primary RAW Jamo now allows wrist gear while blocking elbow gear
- Relaxed Tees no longer receive conflicting turtlenecks, ski masks or balaclavas
- Fixed later recolors, outfit changes and removals bypassing compatibility corrections
- Remove Specific Item(s) now recognizes selected gear even after recoloring
- Cutoff pants now account for leg tattoos added during the same run
- Lower Rolled Jersey now preserves existing cutoff pants with a compatible untucked style

Roster Generator

- Now available from Preseason through National Championship week, like the equipment tools
- Improved speed and other physical ratings to better match player positions and roster strength
- Improved the match between player builds and physical ratings while keeping realistic limits
- Fixed Unlocked tattoos incorrectly blocking generation with an additional appearance layers notice
- Save Team Settings now returns to the team list so you can keep editing other teams
- Widened Configure Teams to show all columns in desktop windows, with simpler wording and better alignment

Jersey Renumber

- Added promotion chances for each position

Long Snap Fixer

- Now reorders the long snapper depth chart using tight ends, centers and guards
- Offensive tackles no longer have their Long Snap ratings changed
- Preview now shows long snapper depth chart moves alongside rating changes
