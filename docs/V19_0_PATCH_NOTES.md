# v19.0

- Cleaner tool pages with focused configuration windows instead of long lists of settings.
- Improved helmet controls: set mixes by position group (including offensive line), choose compatible facemasks, and use 0% to exclude a helmet. Vicis helmets are configured through the mix, not a separate switch.
- Customize visor frequency by position group and undershirt colors with presets or your own percentages.
- Tape now matches each player's accessory color by default; team percentage distributions remain available.
- Build a custom Smart Force Win model from any existing preset, adjust its sliders, and optionally limit new force wins per week. Every slider now has a short explanation beside its label.
- Custom models save on your computer and can also be used on Home’s Season Matchup Lines screen. Its search box is wider, and model changes keep your search and selected week.
- Facemask names are readable in full instead of being cut off.
- RAW’s equipment pool includes white and black Nike/Battle skullcaps, with verified in-game names. Mod options recommend CFB27 Unlocked 0.96 and RAW Accessories v2.0.4.
- User-controlled teams are marked in team selectors. NIL protection now lives in Player Filters.
- Commentary options fit their panels better, support name suffixes, and always produce complete reports.
- More detailed debugging reports, reliable large-report writing, and preset checks across the tools.
- Removed the separate Logs page, added the GitHub link to About, and standardized mod names and creator credits.

Equipment Patcher: **v5.5**. Smart Force Win: **v4.5**.

New live-sourced facemasks are optional and start unchecked. Their individual in-game appearances still need testing.
