### Release Notes

General

- Updated Team Boost and Jersey Renumber icons to match the other tools
- Mod requirements now show the mod name without recommended version numbers

Roster Generator

- Added support for standard Play Now roster files
- Updated preview cards to highlight key ratings, hometown, class, archetype, height, weight, development, and captain status. Technical details stay tucked away
- Added a rare chance for generated teammates to be brothers, sharing a last name, skin tone, and hometown
- Generated dynasty rosters now redistribute the team's existing player NIL costs without changing the total. Quarterbacks generally receive a larger share

Equipment Mapper (NEW)

- Browse and search equipment already present in your save, including custom gear, with usage counts and lists of players wearing each item
- Replace matching items or copy equipment from a specific player, with team, position, and individual-player targeting
- Queue multiple changes, save reusable mapping presets, and review everything before applying
- Copy or replace gloves, cleats, and arm sleeves as left/right pairs. Gloves use paired copying by default
- Set a replacement percentage for each rule, applied separately to each team's matching players
- Matching items are grouped together to reduce clutter, with readable categories and optional saved-configuration details
- Uses the Toolkit's compatibility checks, backups, and save verification

Equipment Randomizer

- Added an optional ELJAX's Equipment Truck pool by ElJax YT, including all three sleeve designs in four colors and both team-branded visors
- Configure allowed items and separate sleeve and visor chances

Equipment Patcher

- Recolor Accessories now supports all four colors of Equipment Truck sleeves while preserving their design
