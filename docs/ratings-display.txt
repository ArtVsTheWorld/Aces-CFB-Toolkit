RATINGS DISPLAY

Settings > Ratings Display > Show Ratings As is an app-wide preference. Numbers
is the compatible default for new and existing settings. No save selection is
required to change it. It is separate from tool configurations and presets.

Source policy reviewed November 16, 2026:
https://github.com/ArtVsTheWorld/BeyondTheField/blob/main/Source/EnhancementOptions.h
https://github.com/ArtVsTheWorld/BeyondTheField/blob/main/Source/RecruitingGradePolicy.h

Numbers: exact existing displays, preserving their requested decimal precision.
Letters: A >=90, B >=80, C >=70, D >=60, F <60.
Letters +/−: F below60. Each passing decade uses minus at0–2, plain at3–6,
plus at7–9. A+ is97–100.
S Tier: the Letters scale, with S at95+.
Words: Elite >=90, Great >=80, Good >=70, Fair >=60, Poor <60.
Hidden: en dash (–) for all ratings, including unknown values.
Other protected modes use ? for an unavailable rating. Toolkit averages retain
their containing band, rather than rounding a fractional average up a grade.
Partially known ranges use the floored low and ceiled high endpoints, as BTF does.

This only changes visible presentation. Source values, editing plans, preset
configuration, numeric inputs, scope tests, donor selection/sorting, report CSVs,
review snapshots and game saves keep exact numeric values. No ratings are written
by changing this setting. Existing saved reviews use the CURRENT display setting.
Switching modes does not clear reviewed plans or regenerate players.

Covered surfaces include equipment and jersey previews, player selector labels,
Roster Generator cards/tables/all-rating details, position/depth averages and
tooltips, Configure Teams, Team Boost old/new values and room averages, team
OVR/OFF/DEF badges, relevant summaries, and the corresponding saved reviews.
Exact per-player Team Boost deltas become direction arrows in protected modes.
Raw roster-talent/unit rating differences in Force Win details are withheld in
protected modes. Betting projections, statistics, ages, shirt numbers, IDs,
money, heights/weights and non-rating counters retain their original meaning.

Deliberate scope: this does not import BTF dynasty preferences or scouting state,
change the game's display, add grade colors, or conceal development traits and
abilities. No guessed scouting uncertainty is invented from a known save rating.
Reports can reveal exact values, as the user explicitly requested; Settings
explains that. This is spoiler-friendly presentation, not a security boundary.

Implementation: shared pure ratingsDisplay.cjs policy, renderer formatting at
identified display sites, validated settings IPC and persisted preference. No
formatting is performed in rating generation, save writing or report generation.
