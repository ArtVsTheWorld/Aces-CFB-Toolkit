### Release Notes

General

- Added an app-wide Ratings Display setting with Beyond the Field-style letter grades, +/− grades, S tier, descriptive labels and hidden ratings. Numbers remain the default, and CSV exports stay numeric

Equipment Tools

- Quickstart, Randomizer, Patcher and Mapper can now run anytime, including the offseason
- Cutoff pants now remove existing towels, and equipment pools cannot add towels to players wearing cutoff pants

Equipment Mapper

- Added equipment source labels throughout browsing, selections, rules and previews to distinguish CFB 27 Equipment, Unlocked, RAW Accessories and GameDay Gear. Sources can also be searched
- Cleaned up Guardian Cap, Skullcap and Dreadlock categories and corrected their equipment slot selectors
- Review Fix now addresses conflicts caused by your requested changes while leaving unrelated existing equipment conflicts alone
- Identical proposed fixes across different outfits are now grouped together, with correct player counts and individual review choices preserved
- Removed the invalid Unlocked White Compression Tee entry. The vanilla White Compression Tee and Irish Jamo Sleeves White remain available. Old rules selecting the removed item explain the issue and require reselection

Roster Generator

- Now assigns four captains per generated team, normally two offensive and two defensive starters, favoring ratings, experience and leadership
- Reduced temporary allocations during OVR calculations while keeping the existing formulas unchanged
